# Confidence guide

Choose a confidence level based on the evidence, not how plausible a statement sounds. Revisit it when new sources appear.

Confirmed
Supported directly by an authoritative primary source.

Corroborated
Supported by multiple independent sources.

Probable
Strong indirect evidence exists.

Possible
Some evidence exists but requires verification.

Unverified
Lead only.

Contradicted
Reliable sources disagree.
